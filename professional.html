Sneak peek

More than 250 red flags across 42 sites, each tagged so you can scan for what you're most likely to miss. A few from outside Breast:

Commonly missed Prostate
ADT started in the urology office

The first leuprolide shot is often given weeks before RT, in a clinic visit, not the cancer center. It is first-course hormone therapy.

Rule to remember Colon
Tumor deposits are not nodes

Tumor deposits go in their own field and can make an N1c. Do not add them to the positive node count.

Coding trap Lung (NSCLC)
SBRT recorded as surgery

“Stereotactic ablation” is radiation. Surgery of primary site stays 00 with the reason no surgery was done.

Commonly missed Melanoma
Adjuvant immunotherapy for stage II

Stage IIB–IIC patients now get adjuvant pembrolizumab or nivolumab. It is easy to miss if you expect adjuvant therapy only for stage III.

Coding trap Rectum
Watch-and-wait is not refusal

When the team chooses observation after a complete response, no-surgery is not a patient refusal. Code the reason accordingly.

Rule to remember Head & Neck
Subsite decides the chapter

Base of tongue is oropharynx; oral tongue is oral cavity. Use the STR Head and Neck site tables, not the clinician’s general term.

Breast · one of 42 sites

Breast

Know before abstracting treatment:
ER PR HER2 (IHC/ISH) Ki-67 Grade Oncotype DX RS Germline BRCA1/2

Subtype drives everything. Identify HR status and HER2 first, then clinical T and N, then decide whether systemic therapy should come before surgery.

Typical first course by stageCommon patterns, and where to confirm them in the chart
Stage 0DCIS
Typical first course
  • Lumpectomy plus whole-breast radiation, or mastectomy
  • Radiation may be omitted for select small, low-grade DCIS
  • Endocrine therapy offered for ER+ DCIS to lower future risk
Look for in the chart
  • Re-excision for close or positive margins (still first course)
  • Med onc or surgeon note offering tamoxifen or an AI
  • SLNB normally only with mastectomy
I–II · HR+/HER2−most common pathway
Typical first course
  • Surgery first: lumpectomy + RT, or mastectomy
  • Axillary staging with SLNB; omitting SLNB is now accepted for carefully selected early HR+/HER2− patients
  • Genomic assay (Oncotype) decides whether chemo is added for node-negative and limited node-positive disease
  • Adjuvant endocrine therapy for 5–10 years; CDK4/6 inhibitor (abemaciclib or ribociclib) added for higher-risk disease
  • Adjuvant olaparib for high-risk germline BRCA carriers
Look for in the chart
  • Recurrence score in the path addendum or a separate report
  • Endocrine start date in med onc notes, often after radiation ends
  • Radiation omission note for older patients on endocrine therapy
HER2+any HR status
Typical first course
  • cT2+ or node-positive: neoadjuvant chemo with trastuzumab + pertuzumab, then surgery
  • Residual disease at surgery: switch to T-DM1 (ado-trastuzumab emtansine)
  • Complete response: finish a year of HER2-directed therapy
  • Small node-negative tumors: surgery first, then weekly paclitaxel + trastuzumab
  • HR+ as well: add endocrine therapy after chemo
Look for in the chart
  • Pre-treatment HER2 IHC/ISH on the core biopsy
  • ypT/ypN on the surgical path
  • Switch to T-DM1 documented in med onc plan
Triple negativeER−, PR−, HER2−
Typical first course
  • cT2+ or node-positive: neoadjuvant chemo + pembrolizumab, then pembrolizumab continues after surgery
  • Residual disease: capecitabine, or olaparib if germline BRCA+
  • Small cT1 N0: surgery first is reasonable, adjuvant chemo by size
Look for in the chart
  • Pembrolizumab across both phases (one first course)
  • Capecitabine start after surgery
  • Germline testing result in genetics note
Stage IIIlocally advanced
Typical first course
  • Neoadjuvant systemic therapy by subtype
  • Mastectomy or lumpectomy with axillary surgery
  • Post-mastectomy or regional nodal radiation
  • Adjuvant therapy by subtype and residual disease
Look for in the chart
  • Inflammatory features (cT4d) documented clinically
  • Clinical response on imaging before surgery
Stage IVde novo metastatic
Typical first course
  • Systemic therapy guided by HR, HER2, and repeat biomarkers (PIK3CA, ESR1, BRCA, HER2-low)
  • HR+: endocrine therapy + CDK4/6 inhibitor is the usual first line
  • HER2+: taxane + trastuzumab + pertuzumab remains preferred first line
  • Surgery or RT to the primary only for symptom control or select cases
Look for in the chart
  • Bone-modifying agents are supportive care, not first-course treatment
  • Palliative RT to bone is still radiation; code it
Registry coding tie-insSSDIs, drug categories, and CoC quality measures
Site-specific data items

ER, PR, HER2 summary (plus IHC/ISH values), Ki-67, Oncotype DX recurrence score and risk level, Grade clinical / pathological / post-therapy, Response to Neoadjuvant Therapy, number of positive axillary level I–II nodes.

Systemic therapy categories

Chemotherapy: anthracyclines, taxanes, capecitabine, trastuzumab, pertuzumab, T-DM1, CDK4/6 inhibitors, olaparib. Hormone: tamoxifen, AIs, goserelin/leuprolide for ovarian suppression. Immunotherapy: pembrolizumab.

Surgery and radiation

SLNB vs ALND changes Scope of Regional LN Surgery. Capture reconstruction as the current STORE breast surgery instructions direct for the diagnosis year. Oophorectomy for hormonal suppression goes in the endocrine procedures field, not surgery of primary site.

CoC quality measures
BCSdxBneoCTBnoLN

Measure definitions and criteria are maintained by the American College of Surgeons. See CoC Quality of Care Measures or RCRS.

Red flags and common missesCommonly missedRule to rememberCoding trap
Commonly missed
Endocrine therapy closed out too early

Hormone therapy often starts after radiation, weeks or months after surgery. Leaving it blank because the abstract was done at discharge is the most common breast miss.

Commonly missed
Post-neoadjuvant escalation

T-DM1, capecitabine, or olaparib after residual disease are part of the first course. Read the first med onc visit after surgery.

Rule to remember
HER2-low is HER2 negative

IHC 1+ or 2+/ISH-negative is coded as HER2 negative even when notes say “HER2-low” and the patient later gets trastuzumab deruxtecan.

Rule to remember
No SLNB can be the right answer

For older patients with small HR+/HER2− tumors, skipping axillary surgery is an accepted approach. Do not query the surgeon as a missing procedure.

Coding trap
Refusal of endocrine therapy

Patients often decline or stop AIs. If it was recommended and declined, code refused. Stopping early after starting is still “given.”

Coding trap
Grade from the right specimen

Clinical grade comes from the core biopsy; post-therapy grade from the surgical specimen after neoadjuvant treatment. Keep them in separate fields.
