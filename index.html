    Finding Home in the Chaos

    A reflective journal companion to Chasing Chaos, Finding Home. Try a few prompts here.

    Begin here

    I am not here because I have it all figured out. I do not. I am still sifting through the random thoughts, the memories that show up out of nowhere, and the parts of my story I am still trying to understand.

    Think of these pages like an hourglass. Dump the messy stuff in. The questions. The people. The moments you cannot stop replaying. Then sit with what slowly rises to the top. Maybe it is clarity. Maybe it is just one honest sentence. That counts, too.

    This is not therapy. It is just a place to be real, imperfect, and a little less alone.

    Jenn

    • Follow the feelingChoose the prompt that feels most alive, even if it surprises you.
    • Give yourself permissionWrite one sentence, fill the page, skip a question, or come back later.
    • Let it be unfinishedNot every memory needs a lesson. Not every feeling needs a solution.
    • Make it yoursPhotographs, ticket stubs, prayers, lists, doodles, or blank space. It all belongs.

    Nobody Should Have to Figure It Out Alone

    A place to ask, to share, and to keep moving together.

    This started on Facebook with a simple idea: most of us make our biggest decisions while we're still uncertain. It helps to know you're not the only one. The conversation lives in the Figuring It Out Facebook group, where you can ask for a nudge, answer someone else's question, cheer each other on, or share the thing you finally did.

    How I Figure Things Out

    Not a formula I learned. A practice I found by looking back.

    1. Question everythingQuestions become the starting point.
    2. Look closerGet curious instead of staying frustrated.
    3. Make sense of the piecesPatterns appear. Dots connect.
    4. Apply what you learnSome things work. Some don't. Both teach.
    5. Build confidence through actionEvery small win is proof.
    6. Keep moving through itProgress isn't always visible.
    7. Share what you knowBecause you remember being lost.

    You don't move because you've figured it out. You figure it out because you choose to keep moving.

    Questions → Curiosity → Learning → Understanding → Growth → Keep Moving

    Movement provokes more questions.

    Hi, I'm Jenn.

    I've spent my life figuring it out as I go.

    Sometimes gracefully. Usually not. I'm the author of Chasing Chaos, Finding Home, a book I wrote one memory, one poem, and one story at a time.

    For more than thirty years I've worked in cancer registry, turning messy, scattered information into something clear and trustworthy. Somewhere along the way I realized the same process works on life: ask the question, look closer, make sense of the pieces, and keep moving.

    When I'm not working, you'll probably find me on the road in our motorhome looking for a campsite with a good sunset, on horseback, or at the pool table with my league team, lining up a shot I was sure I had.

    • HappiestBy a campfire, watching the sun go down
    • WeekendsThe open road, a horse, or a pool cue
    • Day job30+ years in cancer registry
    • AlwaysAsking one more question

    I don't have all the answers. I just keep figuring it out. And I believe you can too.
